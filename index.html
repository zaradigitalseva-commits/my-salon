About MY SALON / MY SALON के बारे में

A platform to discover local beauty and personal-care services / स्थानीय ब्यूटी और पर्सनल-केयर सेवाओं को खोजने का प्लेटफॉर्म

English: MY SALON is an online discovery platform designed to help people find salons and beauty-service providers in their area. It brings salon information together in one place so visitors can explore available businesses, understand the services they list, and contact a salon directly for more details.

हिंदी: MY SALON एक ऑनलाइन प्लेटफॉर्म है, जहाँ लोग अपने आसपास के सैलून और ब्यूटी सेवाएँ देने वाले व्यवसायों की जानकारी खोज सकते हैं। इसका उद्देश्य सैलून की जानकारी एक जगह उपलब्ध कराना है, ताकि ग्राहक सेवाएँ समझ सकें और अधिक जानकारी के लिए सीधे संबंधित सैलून से संपर्क कर सकें।

What customers can do / ग्राहक क्या कर सकते हैं

English: Visitors can browse salon listings and search using details such as salon name, area, city or state. Depending on the information provided by each salon, a listing may include its location, address, contact details, opening hours, photographs, services and prices. Customers should confirm current prices, availability, timings and appointment requirements with the salon before visiting. MY SALON helps people discover and contact businesses; it does not itself perform salon treatments or guarantee a salon's service quality.

हिंदी: ग्राहक सैलून की सूची देख सकते हैं और सैलून का नाम, इलाका, शहर या राज्य लिखकर खोज सकते हैं। सैलून द्वारा दी गई जानकारी के अनुसार लिस्टिंग में पता, संपर्क विवरण, खुलने का समय, फोटो, सेवाएँ और कीमतें दिखाई जा सकती हैं। जाने से पहले ग्राहक संबंधित सैलून से कीमत, उपलब्धता, समय और अपॉइंटमेंट की पुष्टि कर लें। MY SALON व्यवसायों को खोजने और उनसे संपर्क करने में मदद करता है; यह स्वयं सैलून सेवाएँ प्रदान नहीं करता और न ही किसी सेवा की गुणवत्ता की गारंटी देता है।

For salon owners / सैलून मालिकों के लिए

English: Salon owners can submit a registration request with their business and contact information. Submitting a form does not automatically make a salon public or LIVE. The submitted details are subject to review and approval by the platform administrator. Owners are responsible for keeping their listing accurate and up to date, including their contact information, address, operating hours, services and prices. They should only upload photographs and information they are authorised to use.

हिंदी: सैलून मालिक अपने व्यवसाय और संपर्क की जानकारी देकर रजिस्ट्रेशन अनुरोध भेज सकते हैं। फॉर्म जमा करने से सैलून अपने-आप सार्वजनिक या LIVE नहीं होता। जानकारी की जाँच और प्लेटफॉर्म एडमिन की मंज़ूरी के बाद ही लिस्टिंग सक्रिय की जाती है। मालिक अपनी लिस्टिंग में पता, संपर्क नंबर, समय, सेवाएँ और कीमतें सही तथा अपडेट रखें। केवल वही फोटो और जानकारी अपलोड करें जिनका उपयोग करने का उन्हें अधिकार है।

Our approach / हमारा उद्देश्य

English: We aim to make local salon discovery straightforward, provide useful business information, and give independent salons an opportunity to build an online presence. We encourage visitors to make informed choices, compare the details available, and speak with the salon directly whenever they need clarification.

हिंदी: हमारा उद्देश्य स्थानीय सैलून खोजना आसान बनाना, उपयोगी व्यवसाय जानकारी उपलब्ध कराना और छोटे-बड़े सैलून को ऑनलाइन पहचान बनाने का अवसर देना है। हम ग्राहकों को जानकारी देखकर सोच-समझकर चुनाव करने और किसी भी शंका के लिए सैलून से सीधे बात करने के लिए प्रोत्साहित करते हैं।

Salon & Beauty Services / सैलून और ब्यूटी सेवाएँ

Explore common services you may find at salons listed on MY SALON. Each salon independently decides which services it offers, its prices and appointment availability.

MY SALON helps visitors discover local businesses that provide beauty, grooming and personal-care services. The categories below are general examples to help you understand what to look for. Not every salon offers every service. Please check the individual salon profile and contact the salon to confirm exact details before booking or visiting.

Haircut & Hair Styling / हेयरकट और हेयर स्टाइलिंग

Salons may offer haircuts, trimming, blow-drying, styling and occasion-based hair arrangements. Ask the salon about hair length, styling options, estimated duration and charges.

Hair Care / बालों की देखभाल

Depending on the salon, services may include hair wash, conditioning, hair spa, colouring or other hair-care treatments. Discuss your requirements and ask about products, process and pricing before choosing a service.

Makeup & Occasion Styling / मेकअप

Some salons provide everyday, party, bridal or event makeup. Check the salon's portfolio where available and confirm the appointment time, products, package details and final price directly.

Skin & Facial Care / त्वचा और फेशियल

Salons may list facials, clean-ups and other cosmetic skin-care services. Service suitability differs from person to person; discuss ingredients and any concerns with the provider before proceeding.

Manicure & Pedicure / मैनीक्योर और पेडीक्योर

These services may include nail shaping, basic nail care and hand or foot grooming. Ask what is included, how tools are cleaned and whether an appointment is needed.

Grooming & Personal Care / ग्रूमिंग और पर्सनल केयर

Depending on the business, listings may include threading, waxing, beard grooming or other personal-care options. Confirm availability, hygiene practices and charges with the salon.

How to find the right salon / सही सैलून कैसे चुनें

  1. Search by salon name, area, city or state.
  2. Read the listing carefully and review the address, photographs and service details provided.
  3. Compare the information available from different salons.
  4. Contact your preferred salon to confirm current prices, timings, availability and appointment requirements.
  5. Visit only after you are comfortable with the details shared by the salon.

Service names and descriptions are for general information only. Actual services, results, prices, packages and availability are determined by each salon and may change. MY SALON does not provide medical advice or replace consultation with a qualified professional.

Privacy Policy / गोपनीयता नीति

MY SALON · Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MY SALON ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you visit our website, browse salon listings, sign in, register a salon or send an appointment request. Please read it carefully. This policy describes the website's current features; third-party services may have their own terms and privacy policies.

यह नीति बताती है कि MY SALON वेबसाइट पर आने, सैलून खोजने, Google से लॉगिन करने, सैलून रजिस्टर करने या अपॉइंटमेंट अनुरोध भेजने पर आपकी जानकारी का उपयोग कैसे किया जा सकता है।

1. Information we collect / जानकारी जो हम प्राप्त करते हैं

Account and sign-in information: When you choose Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication processes sign-in information and provides account details such as your Google account identifier, email address and, where available, display name and profile information.

Salon registration information: Salon owners may submit salon name, owner name, mobile number, area, city, state, address and an optional salon photograph. The website stores registration details together with account identifiers, registration status, payment status and relevant timestamps so the request can be reviewed and managed.

Appointment request information: When a signed-in customer submits an appointment request, the website records the selected salon and service, listed price, requested date and time, customer account identifier, display name, mobile number supplied for the request, request status and creation time.

Salon and service information: Published salon profiles may contain business contact details, address, opening hours, photographs, service names, descriptions, prices and offers supplied or managed by the salon.

Technical information: The hosting provider and third-party services may process technical information needed to deliver, secure and troubleshoot the website, such as IP address, browser or device information and request logs, according to their own practices. We do not currently add a separate Google Analytics tracking tag in this website code.

2. How we use information / जानकारी का उपयोग

Information is used to provide website functions, authenticate users, display approved salon listings, manage salon registration and review, administer subscription/payment verification records, show salon services and offers, submit and manage appointment requests, support customer-to-salon communication, respond to enquiries, maintain security, prevent misuse and troubleshoot errors.

3. Public information and salon owners / सार्वजनिक जानकारी

After review and approval, salon business details may be displayed publicly so visitors can discover and contact the salon. Public details may include salon name, business address or area, phone or WhatsApp contact, opening hours, photographs, services, prices and offers. Salon owners should submit only information and images they are authorised to publish. Do not include personal or confidential information in public business fields.

सैलून मालिक अपनी लिस्टिंग की जानकारी सही और अपडेट रखने के लिए जिम्मेदार हैं। सार्वजनिक लिस्टिंग में दी गई जानकारी वेबसाइट के सभी विज़िटर्स को दिखाई दे सकती है।

4. Appointment requests / अपॉइंटमेंट अनुरोध

An appointment request is stored in the website database and may be visible to the relevant salon and authorised platform administrators for handling the request. The website may open WhatsApp with a pre-filled appointment message so the customer can contact the salon. WhatsApp is a separate service; if you choose to continue, its own terms and privacy policy apply. A request is not a confirmed appointment until the salon confirms it directly.

5. Payments and verification information / भुगतान की जानकारी

Where salon registration or renewal payments are handled through the website's manual verification process, information such as payment status, UTR/reference details and a payment proof link or image, if submitted, may be stored and reviewed by authorised administrators. Do not submit card PINs, passwords, OTPs or other banking credentials. MY SALON does not request these credentials through its salon registration form. Payment processing or UPI apps used by you are separate services governed by their own policies.

6. Firebase and service providers / तकनीकी सेवा प्रदाता

The current website uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication for Google sign-in and Cloud Firestore for application records. These services process information to provide login, database and related website functionality. The website also loads Google-hosted Firebase libraries and a Google Translate script for language translation. These third-party services may process technical data when your browser connects to them. Please review the providers' own privacy information:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Translate information: https://translate.google.com/
  • WhatsApp Privacy Policy: https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy

7. Cookies and similar technologies / कुकीज़

We do not currently operate a separate cookie-consent banner or intentionally install a standalone advertising-cookie system through the website code. However, Google sign-in, Firebase, hosting infrastructure, embedded or linked third-party services and browser features may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for authentication, security, service delivery or their own functions. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may prevent sign-in or some website features from working properly.

8. Advertising and Google AdSense / विज्ञापन

Google AdSense is not currently integrated in the website code reviewed for this policy. If advertising services such as Google AdSense are added in the future, those providers may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to serve, measure and, depending on applicable settings and consent requirements, personalise advertisements. This policy will be updated to describe the advertising technology actually enabled. For information about Google's advertising practices, visit https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can review Google's available ad-personalisation controls at https://myadcenter.google.com/.

हम विज्ञापन कुकीज़ सक्रिय होने का दावा नहीं करते जब तक वेबसाइट पर संबंधित विज्ञापन सेवा वास्तव में जोड़ी न जाए।

Appointment information may be made available to the relevant salon and authorised administrators. We may disclose information if required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, the website or legal rights. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

10. Data storage, retention and security / डेटा संग्रह, अवधि और सुरक्षा

Information is stored using the website's configured Firebase and hosting services. We retain records for as long as reasonably needed to operate the platform, manage listings and appointment requests, maintain business and verification records, resolve disputes, protect the service or meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Your choices and requests / आपके विकल्प और अनुरोध

You may choose whether to sign in, whether to submit a salon registration request and whether to send an appointment request. You may contact the relevant salon to correct its public business details. For account or registration information handled by MY SALON, you may submit a request through the website's available contact or administrative route. We may need to verify your identity and may retain certain records where required for legitimate operational or legal purposes.

12. Children's privacy / बच्चों की गोपनीयता

MY SALON is a general salon-discovery website and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Children should use the website only with appropriate parent or guardian guidance. A parent or guardian who believes a child has submitted personal information may contact us through the website's contact route.

13. External links / बाहरी लिंक

Our website may link to salon websites, WhatsApp, Google services or other third-party websites. We do not control those services and are not responsible for their content, security or privacy practices. Review the privacy policy of each external service before sharing information with it.

14. Changes to this policy / नीति में बदलाव

We may revise this policy when website features, service providers or applicable legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Please check this page periodically.

15. Contact / संपर्क

For questions or privacy-related requests about MY SALON, please use the Contact page on our website. For questions about a salon's own services, appointment or business information, please contact that salon directly using the contact details shown on its listing.
