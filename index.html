Privacy Policy

MY SALON respects your privacy. This page explains what information may be collected when you use our website.

Information we collect

When customers, salon owners or administrators use MY SALON, we may process information they provide, such as name, email address, phone number, salon details, service information and appointment enquiries.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the platform, display salon listings, help customers contact salons, manage salon registrations and payments, respond to enquiries, and maintain website security.

Sharing

Information needed to make a salon listing or appointment enquiry work may be shown to the relevant customer, salon or platform administrator. We do not publish private account credentials.

Storage and security

Some information is stored using service providers that support website hosting, authentication and database functions. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about your information or request correction of details you supplied, subject to applicable requirements.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the contact details provided on our Contact page.

This policy may be updated when the website or its practices change.
